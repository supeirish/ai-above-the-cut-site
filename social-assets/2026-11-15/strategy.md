## 1 · LinkedIn newsletter companion (Sunday night)

**Headline:** AI agents are knocking. Who opens the door?

**Subtitle:** What stood out to me this week, and the full list of headlines.

*AI Above the Cut is my free weekly newsletter on the AI news that matters for business leaders. Subscribe at aiabovethecut.com/subscribe.*

[Heading] What happened

On Friday, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet with American Express, Mastercard and Visa. An AI agent can now pay with a stand-in card number, and you approve each purchase with a check like Face ID. It starts with a small group of US Pro users.

The same week, United said it will restrict outside AI agents from booking, citing risks like unintended purchases and account changes. And the biggest US banks already block agents from customer accounts.

[Heading] Why it caught my eye

The traffic is tiny. Airbnb's chief executive called agent bookings "a very non-material number." Salesforce's own survey found only 19% of people who use a personal shopping agent have let it check out.

So this is not a fight over volume yet. It is a fight over the rules: who gets to say an agent was allowed to buy, and who pays when it gets it wrong. Insurers are already writing generative AI exclusions into general liability policies.

My read: the companies that sort out permission early will set the terms for everyone else.

[Heading] This week's headlines
